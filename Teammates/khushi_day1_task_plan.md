# Khushi — Short Day 1 Task Plan

## Main responsibility

Khushi is the **main speaker** for PastPort India. Day 1 is only for understanding the project and preparing the speaking points. **She does not need to make the PPT on Day 1.**

## Day 1 tasks

1. Understand the SIH26195 problem and PastPort India’s purpose.
2. Learn the difference between the three app experiences:
   - **3D:** rotate and zoom the monument on the screen.
   - **AR:** see the virtual monument through the phone camera in the real surroundings.
   - **VR:** enter an immersive virtual experience on a compatible device.
3. Understand the basic user flow:

```text
Search Taj Mahal → Read history → Explore 3D → View in AR → Optional VR
```

4. Prepare a short speaking outline covering:
   - the problem;
   - the target users;
   - the PastPort India solution;
   - why AR and VR are useful; and
   - the expected cultural impact.
5. Ask Priyansh, Siddhant, and Shreyas for confirmed technical details before mentioning them in the future presentation.
6. Spend a maximum of 30 minutes becoming familiar with the app structure and demo flow.

## What she should not do on Day 1

Khushi should **not create PPT slides, edit the PPT file, write a complete presentation, or spend a long time on presentation design** on Day 1.

She should not change:

```text
AR files
VR files
3D/model files
frontend files owned by Shreyas
backend or database files
package.json or lock files
.env files
```

If she wants to try coding for learning, she must first ask Priyansh or Shreyas and edit only a small, approved file. Her learning must not disturb Siddhant’s AR work or the shared AR + VR product structure.

## Suggested Day 1 time limit

| Time | Task |
|---|---|
| 20 minutes | Understand the problem and solution |
| 20 minutes | Learn 3D, AR, and VR differences |
| 20 minutes | Understand the user journey |
| 20 minutes | Write speaking points in a personal document |
| 10 minutes | Ask the technical team for confirmed facts |

**Total:** Approximately 90 minutes.

## Day 1 output

Khushi only needs to provide:

- a short personal speaking outline;
- a clear explanation of 3D, AR, and VR;
- the PastPort India user journey; and
- a list of technical details she needs later.

The actual PPT preparation will begin on a later day after the technical team has confirmed the screenshots, features, and demo flow.
